To the Editor-in-Chief,
IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing

Please consider “Selective Recovery of Prompt-Resident Facts through Tool-Schema
Parameters: A Controlled Measurement Study” as a regular paper.

The manuscript measures whether a third-party tool parameter selects a matching
confidential fact from several simultaneously planted facts. It reports a paired
field-level comparison on two protocol-named models, substantial field
heterogeneity, negative controls, and explicit distinctions between prospective,
exploratory and incomplete evidence. Parameter-based extraction is established
prior art; our contribution is its selective-recovery measurement and a
reproducible account of the tested conditions and analytical limitations.

The work addresses TDSC's security measurement and evaluation scope. Historical
observations concern emitted arguments and do not establish server-received
exfiltration, real-world prevalence or an operational defense. The supplementary
file provides completeness accounting, sensitivity analyses and reproduction
instructions. Post-collection implementation corrections are disclosed.
