# Expanded journal manuscript

`main.tex` is the journal manuscript: **Required Tool Parameters Select
Confidential Prompt Facts: Schema Validity, Field Type, and Content Matching Do
Not Govern Their Release**. `supplement.tex` is its Supplementary Material and holds the pilot
studies. The separate focused IEEE version in `../journal_paper/` is older and
is not overwritten.

**Current structure (2026-10-04).** One section per study, each with a design,
results and boundary: Study 1 (fact-by-field matrix, preregistered), Study 2
(schema constraints and typed fields at handler receipt, preregistered), Study 3
(encodings and typed slots against a dispatch filter, preregistered, opening
with the policy replay over the Study 2 calls), and Study 4 (exploratory: three
newer models, planted user and working-directory facts, and a tool that refuses
the reduced call). Robustness checks, detection and the failed hypothesis follow
in one section, then ecological context, discussion, threats to validity and
related work. Eight figures are in `main.tex` (six in the body, two in the
detection appendix) and three in `supplement.tex`. The source names its venue,
`\journal{Journal of Information Security and Applications}`.
The two documents cite each other through `main_labels.tex` and
`supplement_labels.tex`, written by `code/cross_labels.py`. The round-2 review is
in `../reviews/q1_review_2026-10-04_writing_revision.md`. The paragraphs below
record earlier passes; section numbers in them refer to older layouts.

**Restructured on 2026-10-01 (restructure pass).** The main text is now 13 pages
and leads with two studies: Study 1 (the fact-by-field matrix, formerly "v3") and
Study 2 (schema constraints and typed fields, formerly "Experiment 25"; Study 2a
is stage A and Study 2b is stage B). Results begin on page 5. The pilot studies

## Evidence and editorial treatment

The manuscript leads with selective recovery in emitted arguments. It preserves
all major historical comparisons while correcting overstated interpretations:

- Generation, validity, dispatch, receipt and task utility are separate outcomes.
- The paired-analysis correction is disclosed as post-collection work.
- The generic diagonal rule is undefined and the unplanted grid omits two fields.
- Additional providers, the partial Pro arm, familiarity and depth remain exploratory.
- The earlier majority-model rule failed; the runtime self-knowledge claim is withdrawn.
- Reconstructed listings are distinguished from native-schema server execution.
- Scanner votes are provisional because historical raw judge responses are missing.
- Classification and retrospective filtering do not establish operational prevention.

No historical run, protocol, human rating, or submission attestation was changed.
Experiment 25, collected by the authors on 2026-09-29 under its sealed protocol, is
reported as RQ5 from the four canonical legs declared in `code/journal_assets.py`.
It is never pooled with the historical evidence. Existing worksheet
agreement is recomputed from the completed annotations and their key;
no ratings or human attestation are supplied by the assistant. The historical
attestation uses suffixed multi-author fields, which the single-rater validator
rejects. The manuscript reports this unresolved provenance issue and does not
claim that the human-validation requirement is complete.

The selected bibliography uses the original `refs.bib` plus the corrections and
additions in `../journal_paper/additional_refs.bib`, which have an existing local
literature-review record. This revision does not claim a new exhaustive literature
search or an independent audit of every cited source.

## Build and check

```bash
make -C paper
make -C paper check
```

The Makefile prefers `.venv-q1/bin/python`, the repository's locked analysis
environment, and otherwise uses `.venv/bin/python`. An explicit `PY=...` overrides
that choice. The experiment environment in this workspace lacks PyYAML, so the
locked environment is needed for regeneration. Without an environment, the PDF
build uses tracked generated assets; evidence checks require the dependencies.

`code/manifest.py --tex` regenerates result macros and the ROC figure.
`code/paper_assets.py --write` reuses the audited analysis functions to generate:

- `analysis_numbers.tex`;
- `generated_*_table.tex` (including exact descriptions, accounting and sensitivity);
- `journal_refs.bib`, the selected bibliography with reviewed overrides;
- `details_*.tex`, generated by `code/paper_details.py` from retained results and
  fixture definitions, including complete prompts and additional descriptive intervals.
  `details_macros.tex` holds every count and interval that the prose cites and that
  `numbers.tex` does not register.

## Figures

`code/plot_figures.py` draws all ten figures with matplotlib from the same evidence
functions as the tables, writes them to `../figures/*.pdf`, and mirrors them into
`figures/` here so that this folder builds on its own. It also writes
`../figures/plot_data.json`, the values that were plotted. Install the plotting
dependency with `pip install -r requirements-figures.txt` on top of the analysis
environment. `make -C paper` redraws the figures when matplotlib is present and
otherwise uses the committed PDFs. `make -C paper check` fails if the plotted data
no longer matches the evidence, if a mirror is stale, or if a figure has a Type 3
or non-embedded font.

| File | Shows |
|---|---|
| `selectivity` | field-by-fact recovery for the two protocol-named models |
| `matrix` | matched versus nonmatched recovery for five models |
| `gradient` | matched recovery for every field and model |
| `gate` | gate conditions and wording variants |
| `payload` | recovery by payload and condition, including explicit condition E |
| `interfaces` | reconstructed interfaces by outcome |
| `reticence` | the confidentiality ladder |
| `constraints` | Experiment 25: free versus constrained strings, and the capacity arm |
| `roc`, `detector_scores` | classifier ROC, score distribution, and precision by prevalence |

Figures are sized for the final page (252pt column, 522pt text width), use
Liberation Serif at 8pt or larger, and embed their fonts as TrueType.
`code/make_figures.py` still renders the PNGs that `usenix_paper/` includes. It no
longer writes `matrix.pdf`, `gradient.pdf`, or `roc.pdf`.

**Never type a result number into `main.tex`.** `paper_assets.py --check` fails if
the manuscript body contains a literal fraction, bracketed interval, or decimal
percentage. Add the value to `paper_details.macros()` and cite the macro.

`refs_overrides.bib` holds verified corrections for this manuscript only. It is
merged last. `refs.bib` and `../journal_paper/additional_refs.bib` also feed the
focused manuscript, whose generated bibliography is hash-locked, so do not edit
them for this paper.

Do not edit these generated files. Change source evidence or presentation logic
and rebuild. `--check` recomputes their expected contents and detects drift.

The output is `paper/main.pdf`. To compile tracked assets without regeneration:

```bash
cd paper
latexmk -pdf -bibtex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
```

LaTeX needs `elsarticle`, `elsarticle-num.bst`, `amsmath`, `amssymb`, `courier`,
`txfonts`, `microtype`, `xurl`, `booktabs`, `longtable`, `listings`, `pict2e`,
`tikz` (for the threat-model diagram), `xcolor` and `hyperref`. On TinyTeX,
`tlmgr install pgf` supplies `tikz`.

journal-template layout is retained. The template is only the starting layout:
the manuscript targets the **Journal of Information Security and
Applications** (`\journal{...}` in `main.tex`) since 2026-10-05. Computers &
Security was dropped: its Elsevier page excludes work on the security of AI/ML
systems such as LLMs. The JISA guide for authors is still to be read for the
abstract limit, highlights and length (TODO T34).

Author names, affiliations, corresponding email, contributions, funding,
competing interests, final release location/license and the reviewed AI-assistance
statement require actual author information. They are visibly identified in the
draft. No claim that human review or vendor notification is complete was invented.
